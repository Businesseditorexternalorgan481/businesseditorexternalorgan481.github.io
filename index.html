🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

Issue: Application won't start after downloading.

Solution: Make sure you've downloaded the correct file for Windows. If problems persist, try downloading again or restarting your computer.

Issue: Can't find the downloaded file.

Solution: Check your Downloads folder. If it's not there, use your browser's download manager (Ctrl+J in most browsers) to locate the file.

Issue: Slow performance.

Solution: Close other applications running in the background to free up system resources.
